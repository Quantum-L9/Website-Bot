## 3. Defects found and repaired

1. **Sign-flipped delta normalization (30/30 trials).** Every trial's stored
   `normalized_candidate_delta` was the exact negation of the value required
   by the invariant
   (`B==CANDIDATE → raw delta; A==CANDIDATE → −raw delta`).
   `normalized_preference` was correct in all 30 trials. Recomputed from
   `raw_judge + orientation` in `visual/trials-raw.json`,
   `visual/aggregated/normalized-results.json`, and `receipt.json`.
2. **Corrupted aggregate.** `visual/aggregated/aggregate.json` carried
   negated dimension means additionally divided by ~10 (e.g. −0.0067 where
   the corrected mean is +0.0667) and a negated weighted mean (−0.0141).
   Rebuilt from the corrected trials.
3. **Receipt visual schema drift.** `receipt.json` visual trials carried
   `judge_json` instead of `raw_judge`, an orientation *string* instead of
   the orientation object (`{A, B, randomized/reversed_from_trial_1/
   independent}`), judge-input manifests missing the five
   `*_exposed: false` blindness attestations, pairs missing
   `candidate_run_id`, and the visual section missing
   `rendered_visual_qa_executed: true` (visual QA execution is proven by
   `pipeline.log`). All restored to the generator contract
   (`scripts/run-safehaven-golden-visual.ts`); orientation objects were
   rebuilt from the recorded `orientation_map` values.
4. **Non-conforming oracle result.** The committed `golden-oracle-result.json`
   (schema v1, no evaluation coverage) was not produced by
   `scripts/verify-safehaven-golden.mjs` and aggregated the corrupted deltas.
   Regenerated by running the actual verifier against the repaired receipt
   (schema `l9.golden-oracle-result/v2`, oracle evaluation coverage 101/101).
