Storm damage?

Hail and wind damage is often invisible from the ground.

Charlotte's spring and summer storms bruise shingles, lift flashing, and strip granules — damage that's easy to miss until a leak appears. We respond fast, tarp exposed areas to stop further water intrusion, and give you a free, photo-documented inspection you can bring to your insurance company.

Questions

FAQ

Do you offer free inspections?+

Yes. We'll inspect your roof, document what we find with photos, and give you a straight, no-obligation assessment — at no cost.

Are you insured?+

Yes. Safe Haven is fully insured, and we've served the Charlotte area for six years.

What shingle brands do you install?+

We install GAF, CertainTeed, and Atlas shingles in a range of colors and styles, and we'll help you compare options for durability, look, and budget.

What areas do you serve?+

Charlotte, NC and the surrounding towns — Gastonia, Belmont, Lake Wylie, Mount Holly, Huntersville, Matthews, Waxhaw, Concord, and more. Don't see your town? Give us a call.

Do you help with storm damage and insurance?+

We inspect and document storm damage with photos and a written report, provide a detailed estimate, and can meet your insurance adjuster on-site to walk them through what we found. Filing and negotiating the claim itself stays between you and your insurance company.

Is there a warranty?+

Your materials carry the manufacturer's warranty, and our installation work is backed by our own 5-year workmanship warranty.

How do I get started?+

Call or text us, or request a free inspection online. We'll set up a time to take a look and give you an honest assessment.
