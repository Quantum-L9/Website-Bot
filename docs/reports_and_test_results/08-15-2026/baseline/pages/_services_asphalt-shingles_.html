Asphalt Shingle Roofing — FAQ

How long do asphalt shingles last?+

Most asphalt shingle roofs last 20 to 30 years depending on climate, ventilation, and installation quality. Regular inspections help catch small issues before they shorten that lifespan.

What are the signs I need an asphalt shingle repair?+

Curling shingles, missing pieces, granule loss, and interior stains usually mean the roof isn't sealing properly anymore. Catching these early prevents bigger repairs down the line.

Do you install asphalt shingles on commercial buildings?+

Yes. We evaluate the roof's slope and structure to make sure asphalt shingles are the right material before installing on a residential or commercial property.

How do I choose the right shingle style?+

We'll walk you through GAF, CertainTeed, and Atlas options — comparing texture, color, and weight — so you land on a shingle that fits your property and budget.
