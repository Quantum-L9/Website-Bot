Storm and hail damage in Charlotte

Charlotte gets its share of rough weather. Spring and summer bring hail, gusty straight-line winds, and the soggy tail ends of Atlantic systems that push rain sideways under shingles. The tricky part is that hail damage often doesn’t look like much from the driveway — bruised shingles, knocked-off granules, and cracked seals can sit up there quietly shortening the life of your roof until the next hard rain finds them. After a storm rolls through your neighborhood, it’s worth having someone get up top and look closely. That inspection is free, and we’ll show you photos of whatever we find.

Working with your insurance after a storm

If a storm damaged your roof, the insurance side can feel like the confusing part. Here’s how we help: we inspect the roof, document the damage with photos, and provide our own estimate and scope of work. If it’s useful, we can meet your adjuster on-site so everyone is looking at the same roof and the same information. We’ll guide you through what we’re seeing and answer your questions along the way. Your deductible is your responsibility — that’s set by your policy — but the inspection and the roofing scope are our everyday work, and we’ll walk you through them.
