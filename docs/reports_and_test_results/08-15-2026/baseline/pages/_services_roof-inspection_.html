A clear picture of your roof’s condition

Hidden roof damage doesn’t stay hidden for long — it turns into leaks, interior repairs, and unplanned expense. Safe Haven Roofing & Renovations provides roof inspections across Charlotte, NC that give homeowners and business owners a straightforward, photo-documented look at what’s really going on above their heads.

We’re fully insured and bring six years of local roofing experience to every inspection. Our team examines shingles, decking, flashing, attic ventilation, moisture levels, and drainage, and documents what we find with clear photos and plain-language notes — no vague answers, no guesswork. You walk away knowing exactly what condition your roof is in and what, if anything, needs attention.

When to schedule an inspection

Three situations come up most often: buying or selling a home, checking a roof after a storm, and routine annual maintenance. A pre-sale inspection gives buyers and sellers a clear report to work from so nothing surprises anyone at closing. An annual inspection catches small problems — a lifted shingle, a soft spot in the decking — before they become expensive repairs.

If a storm has passed through and you suspect damage, we inspect the roof and document everything we find with photos. From there, we provide our estimate for the repair or replacement work, and if it’s helpful, we’ll meet your insurance adjuster on-site to walk them through what we documented and answer their questions. The claim itself — filing it and working out the settlement — stays between you and your insurance company; our job is to give you and them an accurate, well-documented picture of the roof.

Questions

Roof Inspection — FAQ

When should I get a roof inspection?+

Common times are before buying or selling a home, after a storm, or as part of annual maintenance — catching small issues early is cheaper than waiting for a leak.

What does the inspection cover?+

We examine shingles, decking, flashing, attic ventilation, moisture levels, and drainage, and document anything that needs attention with photos.

Can you help with an insurance claim after storm damage?+

We can inspect your roof, document the damage with photos, and provide our estimate for the repair or replacement. If you'd like, we'll also meet your adjuster on-site to walk them through what we found. Filing and negotiating the claim itself is between you and your insurance company.

Do you offer inspections for real estate transactions?+

Yes. Our reports give buyers and sellers a clear picture of the roof's condition so there are no surprises during closing.
