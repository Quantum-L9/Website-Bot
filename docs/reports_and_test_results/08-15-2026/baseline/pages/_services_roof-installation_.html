A new roofing system, built for Charlotte weather

Loose shingles, recurring leaks, or a roof simply past its service life can put your comfort and your property at risk. Safe Haven Roofing & Renovations installs new roofing systems for homes and businesses across Charlotte, NC using materials built to hold up to local heat, wind, and storms.

We’re fully insured and bring six years of hands-on local experience to every installation. Every project starts with a real inspection — not just a look at the shingles, but the decking, flashing, and ventilation underneath — so the plan we hand you reflects what your roof actually needs. From there we prepare a clear, itemized estimate before anything is scheduled, and we manage the job with the same attention to detail whether it’s a small home or a large commercial property.

Material options and what’s included

You choose from asphalt shingles, metal panels, or flat-roof systems, and we help you compare them for durability, appearance, and budget. Asphalt shingles are a popular, affordable choice with a wide range of colors. Metal roofing offers long-term durability and low maintenance. Flat roofing systems suit commercial buildings that need efficient drainage.

Every installation follows the same process: old materials are removed and the deck is prepared for a solid foundation, moisture barriers and flashing go in to protect against leaks, and each section is fitted and sealed to handle Charlotte’s changing weather. Because we install trusted brands like GAF, CertainTeed, and Atlas, your new roof carries the manufacturer’s material warranty — and our own 5-year workmanship warranty on top of it.

Roof Installation — FAQ

What roofing materials do you install?+

We install asphalt shingles, metal panels, and flat roofing systems, and we'll walk you through the trade-offs in durability, appearance, and cost for your property.

Do you handle both residential and commercial installations?+

Yes. Residential and commercial roofs have different requirements — load capacity, drainage, and building codes vary — and we plan each installation around your property's structure and budget.

What brands of shingles do you use?+

We install GAF, CertainTeed, and Atlas shingles, giving you a range of colors and styles backed by the manufacturer's material warranty.

How long does a new roof installation take?+

Timelines depend on roof size, materials, and weather. We'll give you a realistic schedule as part of your written estimate.
