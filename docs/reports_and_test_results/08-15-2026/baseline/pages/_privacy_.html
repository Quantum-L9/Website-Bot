Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Safe Haven Roofing & Renovations ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: we only collect what we need to give you a quote and do the job.

Information we collect

When you submit our quote form, we collect the information you provide:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your email address (if you provide it)
  • Your town and a description of the job
  • Your preferred way to be contacted
  • Any photos you choose to upload

We also collect basic, privacy-friendly usage statistics (such as page views) to understand how the site is used. These analytics do not use cookies and do not track you across other websites.

How we use it

We use the information you submit only to respond to your request, provide an estimate, schedule and complete the work, and follow up about your job. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.

Service providers

We rely on a few trusted providers to run the site and handle quote requests. Your information may be processed by them solely to deliver these services:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, secure photo storage, spam protection, and analytics
  • Resend — delivering your quote request to us by email

How long we keep it

We keep quote submissions and related photos only as long as needed to respond to your request and complete any work, plus a reasonable period for our records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just reach out using the contact details below.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults arranging home services. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
