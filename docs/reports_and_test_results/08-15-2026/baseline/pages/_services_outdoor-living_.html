Outdoor spaces built to actually get used

A backyard that’s underused or outdated is a missed opportunity, not a fixed feature. Safe Haven Roofing & Renovations designs and builds decks, patios, fences, and retaining walls for homes across Charlotte, NC and the surrounding towns, turning unused outdoor space into somewhere you’ll actually spend time.

We build custom decks sized and styled to fit your home, install paver or poured-concrete patios for dining and lounging, and construct retaining walls that manage sloped or eroding terrain while looking like they belong there. Fencing is available in wood, vinyl, and aluminum for privacy, security, or simply defining the yard. We’re fully insured and bring six years of local experience to every hardscape project, working with you from initial concept through the final build.

Built for how you’ll use it

Every outdoor project starts with a conversation about how you actually want to use the space — entertaining, everyday family time, privacy, or managing a yard that doesn’t drain well. From there we handle layout, materials, and construction with attention to durability, so your deck, patio, fence, or wall holds up through Charlotte’s weather for years, not just one season.

Outdoor Living & Hardscape — FAQ

Do you build both decks and patios?+

Yes. We design and construct custom decks as well as paver and concrete patios, and we'll help you decide which fits your space and how you use it.

Can a retaining wall help with a sloped or eroding yard?+

Yes. Retaining walls manage uneven terrain and slow erosion, and we design them to handle drainage properly so they hold up over time.

What fencing materials do you offer?+

Wood, vinyl, and aluminum, depending on the look, privacy, and durability you're after.

How long does a typical project take?+

It depends on scope — a fence goes up faster than a full deck or patio build. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your estimate.
