Storm damage

Insurance claims, made clearer

A storm-damage claim can feel overwhelming. Our job is to give you and your insurer an accurate, well-documented picture of your roof — so you can move through the process with confidence.

Where we fit in: Safe Haven inspects, documents, provides a detailed estimate, and meets your adjuster on-site. Filing and negotiating your claim is between you and your insurance company — we're your roofer, not a public adjuster, and we never ask you to waive or discount your deductible.

Step by step

How it works

  1. 01

    Free inspection & documentation

    We inspect your roof after the storm, identify hail and wind damage that's often invisible from the ground, and document every finding with clear photos and a written report — at no cost.

  2. 02

    You file your claim

    You file the claim with your insurance company. We give you the photos, written report, and a detailed estimate you need to do it, and we're happy to answer your questions about what we found.

  3. 03

    We meet your adjuster on-site

    When your insurance adjuster comes out, we meet them at your property and walk them through exactly what we found and why — so nothing gets overlooked.

  4. 04

    Clear scope & estimate

    You receive an itemized estimate and scope of work you can compare against your insurer's assessment, in plain language.

  5. 05

    We complete the work

    Once you're ready, we complete the repair or replacement. Your policy deductible is set by your insurer and is the homeowner's responsibility; we'll always give you the price in writing before any work begins.

  6. 06

    Final walkthrough & warranty

    We inspect the finished work with you, document its completion, and provide your 5-year workmanship warranty.
