Fast help after the storm

When hail, high winds, or heavy rain move through Charlotte, a roof can go from sound to leaking in a single afternoon — and the worst damage is often the kind you can’t see from the ground. Safe Haven Roofing & Renovations responds quickly to storm calls across Charlotte, NC and the surrounding towns. If your roof is open to the weather, we tarp the exposed areas to stop further water intrusion while we plan a proper repair.

Charlotte’s spring and summer storm season brings the kind of hail and wind that bruises shingles, lifts flashing, and strips granules — damage that’s easy to miss and expensive to ignore. That’s why a free post-storm inspection matters: it tells you honestly whether your roof was affected before a small problem turns into a soaked ceiling. We’re fully insured and bring six years of local experience to every storm we respond to.

Our role in your insurance claim

Storm repairs often involve an insurance claim, and our role is to give you and your insurer an accurate, well-documented picture of the roof. We inspect the damage, document everything we find with clear photos and a written report, and provide a detailed estimate and scope of the work. If it’s helpful, we’ll meet your insurance adjuster on-site and walk them through exactly what we found and why.

Filing and negotiating the claim itself stays between you and your insurance company — that’s your relationship, not ours. Your deductible is set by your policy and is the homeowner’s responsibility; we’ll always give you a clear written estimate so you know the price before any work starts. For more on how this process works, see our insurance claims page.

Storm Damage & Insurance Documentation — FAQ

How fast can you respond after a storm?+

We prioritize storm calls and aim to get to your property quickly. If your roof is exposed, we can tarp the damaged areas to stop further water from getting in while we plan the full repair.

How do I know if my roof was damaged if I can't see anything?+

Hail and wind damage is often invisible from the ground — bruised or dislodged shingles, lifted flashing, and granule loss are hard to spot from a driveway. A free post-storm inspection is the reliable way to know, and it's especially worth doing during Charlotte's spring and summer storm season.

How does Safe Haven help with my insurance?+

We inspect the damage, document it with photos and a written report, and provide a detailed estimate and scope. If you'd like, we'll meet your insurance adjuster on-site and walk them through exactly what we found. Filing and negotiating the claim itself stays between you and your insurance company. You can read more on our insurance claims page.

Who pays the deductible?+

Your deductible is set by your policy and is the homeowner's responsibility. We'll give you a clear, written estimate up front so you always know the price before any work begins.
