What actually drives the price of a new roof — and how to get one clear, no-obligation number for your Charlotte home.

If you're planning a new roof in Charlotte, the first question is almost always what it will cost. The honest answer is that no roofer can give you a real number without seeing your roof — the price swings a lot with size, pitch, materials, and what's hiding under the old shingles. What we can do here is lay out exactly what drives roof replacement cost, so you know what you're paying for and how to get one clear, no-obligation number for your home. Safe Haven is a fully insured, Charlotte-based roofer with six years on local roofs, and our inspections and quotes are always free.

What goes into a roof replacement price

The biggest driver is simply how much roof you have. Roofers measure in “squares” — one square is 100 square feet — and a bigger roof means more material and more labor. Pitch matters too: a steep roof is slower and harder to work on safely, so it costs more than a low, walkable one. After that it comes down to the material. We install GAF, CertainTeed, and Atlas shingles, and the line you choose — a basic three-tab, a heavier architectural shingle, or a standing-seam metal roof — changes the price meaningfully. If you’re weighing those two, our metal versus shingles guide covers how each one holds up here. Then there’s the old roof itself: tearing off one layer is quicker than tearing off two, and all of it has to be hauled away and disposed of. None of these are add-ons or surprises when they’re accounted for up front — they’re just the real inputs to an honest number.

What we find underneath

A quote you can trust also accounts for what’s hiding under the shingles. Once the old roof is off, we can see the decking — the plywood the shingles are nailed to. If any of it is soft or rotted from an old leak, it gets replaced so your new roof has something solid to sit on. The same goes for the details that make a roof watertight: flashing around chimneys and walls, the valleys where two roof planes meet, pipe boots and vents, drip edge, and ridge venting. Good work in those spots is where a roof earns its keep, and every roof we install is backed by our five-year workmanship warranty on top of the manufacturer’s material warranty.

Repair or replace?

Not every roof needs replacing. If your roof is relatively young and the damage is limited to one area, a repair is usually the smarter spend. But if the shingles are curling and brittle across the whole roof, or you’re patching leak after leak, a full replacement often costs less over time than chasing the next problem. We’ll get on your roof, show you what we see, and give you the honest call — not the most expensive one.

If a storm caused the damage

If hail or wind is behind your roof trouble, insurance may come into play. Here’s how we help: we inspect the roof, document the damage with photos, and provide our own estimate and scope of work. If it’s useful, we can meet your adjuster on-site so everyone is looking at the same roof. We’ll guide you through what we’re seeing and answer your questions. Your deductible is your responsibility — that’s set by your policy. You can read more on our insurance claims and storm damage pages.

Why we don’t post a price

You’ll notice we haven’t put a dollar figure on this page, and that’s on purpose. Every roof is different, and any number we posted online would either be too high for some homes or too low for others — neither helps you. We’d rather get eyes on your actual roof and give you one clear, honest price than have you guess from a range that doesn’t fit your house. That inspection is free, there’s no obligation, and you’ll know exactly what you’re looking at — including how long the job will take.

Roof Replacement Cost in Charlotte, NC — FAQ

How much does a new roof cost in Charlotte?+

It depends on your roof's size and pitch, the material you choose, how many old layers come off, and any decking or permit work. The only accurate number is a free on-site quote — we'll walk your roof and give you one clear price, no obligation.

Is it cheaper to repair my roof than to replace it?+

Usually a targeted repair costs less than a full replacement. But if the roof is near the end of its life or the damage is widespread, replacing it can be the better value. We'll tell you honestly which one your roof needs.

Will insurance cover my roof replacement?+

If a storm caused the damage, it might. We inspect the roof, document what we find, and provide our estimate and scope. We can meet your adjuster on-site and answer questions. Your deductible is your responsibility.

Do you charge for an estimate?+

No. Inspections and quotes are always free, with no obligation.

How long does a roof replacement take?+

It depends on the size and pitch of your roof, how many old layers come off, and what we find in the decking underneath — plus weather and permit timing. We'll walk you through the timeline for your roof before we start.
