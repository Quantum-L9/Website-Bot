Plumbing services

Leak detection and repair

Hidden leaks waste water and rot framing long before a stain appears. We trace them with acoustic sensors and thermal imaging, mark the exact point on the wall, and open only the section that needs the repair.

Once the pipe is fixed we pressure test the whole branch, because a single failed joint is often a sign that neighbouring joints are close behind.

Drain cleaning

Kitchen lines clog with grease and bathroom lines with hair and soap. We clear both with cable machines sized to the pipe, then run a camera through the line so you can see the result for yourself on the screen.

When roots have entered a sewer lateral we show you the footage and explain the options, from periodic cutting to a full liner, along with what each one costs over ten years.

Water heaters

We install tank and tankless units and size them from the number of bathrooms and the people who use them each morning. Every installation includes a new expansion tank where the code requires one and a written record of the settings.

Before the old heater is removed we check the gas line or electrical supply, the venting and the drain pan, so the replacement does not inherit a problem the previous unit was hiding.
